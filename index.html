Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download the games? +

Some of our premium titles require a download for the best graphical experience, while others can be played instantly in your browser via our proprietary cloud gaming technology.

Are the games free to play? +

We offer a diverse mix of free-to-play titles with optional cosmetics, as well as premium titles available for a one-time purchase or through our Playmotion+ subscription model.

What platforms are supported? +

Playmotion games are cross-platform compatible. You can play seamlessly across Windows, macOS, Linux, and supported iOS/Android mobile devices with cross-save enabled.

How does the ranking system work? +

Our top games are ranked dynamically based on concurrent player counts, community ratings, and recent content updates to ensure you're always seeing the most active titles.
