Before you play

Questions, answered

What is PlayMotion?

PlayMotion is a collection of short, easy-to-start games. You will find tycoons, racers, builders and action games in one place, each with its own card on this page.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Press Play on any card and the game opens in your browser, so there is nothing to install.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Games open on phones, tablets and computers. Racing and 3D games can feel best on a larger screen.

How do I find out when a new game arrives?

Follow PlayMotion on YouTube and Instagram. New games are shown there first, and they appear on this page right after.

Can I suggest a game?

Yes. Send your idea in a message on Instagram or leave a comment on a YouTube video.
